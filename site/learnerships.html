Skills that lead to work

Learn, practise, earn

We focus on the critical and scarce skills the economy needs but mainstream education does not offer.

Our accredited programmes combine classroom theory with practical work. At the Itireleng Hub in Orkney, learners can study, stay and gain hands-on exposure in our own workshops and factories.

Programmes run when funding and intakes open. Register your interest below and our training team will have your details when a suitable programme becomes available.

Ways in

Four routes into skills and work

Whether you are starting out, already working, or running a small business, there is a route that fits.

Learnerships and artisan training

Structured, accredited programmes that combine theory and workplace practice, leading to a recognised qualification.

Recognition of Prior Learning

Already skilled but without a certificate? RPL assesses what you know and can do, so your experience counts towards a qualification.

Work-integrated learning, reskilling and upskilling

Fast-track development for employees, so teams keep pace as roles and technology change.

Incubation and mentorship

Mentoring for women, youth, SMMEs and cooperatives, such as the members of the Itireleng Textile Hub, with help to reach markets and funding.

How it works

From application to practice

Each programme has its own entry requirements and selection process, which we explain when an intake opens.

  1. Step one

    Register your interest

    Tell us who you are and which field interests you. We keep your details for upcoming intakes.

  2. Step two

    Assessment and selection

    When a programme opens, candidates who meet its requirements are invited to apply and be assessed.

  3. Step three

    Train and practise

    Learn in the classroom, then build practical skills in our workshops, with mentoring along the way.

For employers

Develop your people with us

We design and deliver learnerships, skills programmes and RPL for organisations that want to grow their own talent, from a single team to a multi-year programme.

  • Accredited programmes with the QCTO and five SETAs
  • Classrooms, accommodation and workshops at the Itireleng Hub
  • Skills audits to find the gaps before training starts

Who we are

A one-stop partner for holistic development: we plan the strategy, train the people and run the production lines they work on.

Mbuso Management Solutions is a black-owned strategy, training and industrialisation firm. Our head office is in Pretoria and our operations are anchored in Matlosana, North West, at our own learning and manufacturing hub.

We call it a closed loop. Learners gain accredited skills, then move straight into industrial work at the Hub, which keeps delivery steady even when markets are not.

Our method

No intervention without diagnosis

Motivation alone does not change an organisation. We measure first, then give people the tools to do the job, then stay to make sure it holds.

  1. Before

    Diagnose

    Organisational health tests, leadership and culture assessments, pre-training assessments, focus groups and interviews.

  2. During

    Intervene

    Facilitation, business simulations, role plays and case studies, formative assessments and personal development plans.

  3. After

    Sustain

    Ongoing coaching, a site visit a month later, follow-up workshops and support through implementation.

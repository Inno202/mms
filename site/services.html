What we do

Three pillars that work alone or together, so one partner can take you from plan, to skilled people, to production.

Pillar one

Training and development

We focus on critical and scarce skills that the mainstream education system does not offer but the economy needs, so learners can earn and employees can grow.

  • Accredited skills programmes
  • Learnerships and artisan training
  • Recognition of Prior Learning
  • Work-integrated learning, reskilling and upskilling
  • Leadership development
  • Entrepreneurial training
  • Student and graduate development workshops
Pillar two

Industrial operations

At the Itireleng Hub, training leads straight to production. Our ventures give learners practical exposure and a path to employment.

  • Textile and clothing production at the Itireleng Textile Hub
  • Industrial bakery operations
  • Woodworking and casket manufacturing
  • Incubation and mentorship for SMMEs and cooperatives
Inside the Hub
Pillar three

Strategy and consulting

Listed entities and established brands trust us with their medium to long-term strategies and the change management that makes them happen.

  • Business model analysis and development
  • Strategy design and implementation
  • Strategic planning facilitation
  • Organisational development and structure design
  • Culture audits and skills audits
  • Job evaluation
  • Leadership coaching and mentorship
  • Project and facility management

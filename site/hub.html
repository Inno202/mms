Where learning becomes work

Umuzimuhle, Orkney

A mining property, given a second life

When mining closures threatened Matlosana, Daddy Khuselo saw an opportunity in a derelict former mine site. Mbuso Management Solutions secured it from a mining house and turned its training rooms, accommodation and workshops into a learning, innovation and manufacturing hub.

Today the Hub gives learners theory, practical exposure and possible employment in one place, through our in-house engineering and manufacturing facilities.

The Hub also has an office block with 20 offices and a retail park, and sits close to a clinic.

Itireleng Textile Hub

A 2 000 m² factory built for quality

The Textile Hub manufactures clothing and textile products to SABS standards, with areas for storage, cutting, machining, branding and embroidery, packing and dispatch.

  • Around 100 women and youth, many already running businesses, are incubated at the Hub
  • Mentoring by experienced industry players on markets, supply chains and SABS quality
  • Formal training towards SETA qualifications in clothing manufacturing and New Venture Creation
  • Help to reach markets and funding through development finance institutions and corporate sponsors
Grade 12 matric incubation camps

Thousands of matric learners hosted since 2019

Our camps support the matric results of the Dr Kenneth Kaunda District, and the Department of Education has recognised the Hub as a preferred Grade 12 camp venue.

The camps have created more than 200 temporary jobs and income for more than 10 local SMMEs. We procure locally, from electrical materials and plumbing to mattresses, security and gardening services.

Partners behind the Hub

The Hub is a platform for development institutions, SETAs and industry to roll out programmes at scale.

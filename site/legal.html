Privacy policy

Mbuso Management Solutions (Pty) Ltd (registration number 2012/074900/07) respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What this website collects

This website does not collect or store personal information itself. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The contact form checks your details in your browser and then opens your own email app with a message addressed to mail@mbusoms.co.za. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. When you do, we receive the details you chose to include: your name, organisation, email address, phone number and message.

The learnership registration form works the same way. If you send it, we receive your name, cellphone number, email address if given, town, field of interest, highest qualification and any message. We keep these details only to contact you about suitable learnerships, and you may ask us to delete them at any time.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange the services you ask about.
  • To contact you about learnerships and training intakes, if you registered your interest.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Third parties

Emails are handled by our email provider. Links to Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps and news websites take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us at mail@mbusoms.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Development that reaches communities

New Horizons Skills Development Project

Our community development wing

New Horizons Skills Development Project is a registered non-profit organisation (NPO 197-584) and the community development arm of Mbuso Management Solutions. It works nationally through community-specific skills programmes.

  • Train youth and women in self-mastery, self-confidence and goal setting
  • Provide sector-based critical and scarce skills that improve employability
  • Build business acumen and creative skills
  • Create innovative solutions for different sectors of the economy
  • Build partnerships for youth and women development

High-impact success stories

2014 to 2017

Mathematics, Science and English project

Our flagship project in Khuma Township, Matlosana, took 60 needy but deserving Grade 9 learners through to Grade 12 with Saturday and holiday classes. After matric, they received financial and non-financial support to study further, and most are now completing their studies.

Since 2019

Grade 12 matric incubation camps

The Hub hosts matric camps for schools across the Dr Kenneth Kaunda District. The camps have created more than 200 temporary jobs and brought income to more than 10 local SMMEs.

Itireleng Textile Hub

Women and youth in manufacturing

Around 100 women and youth are incubated at the Textile Hub, working towards SETA qualifications and learning to produce to SABS quality.

Water and energy skills

Learnerships with SALGA and EW SETA

150 learners from five municipalities enrolled in a one-year water treatment and reticulation learnership with SALGA, and 50 young people trained in cable jointing and termination with the Energy and Water SETA.

Our story

From motivation and training to industrialisation

Daddy Khuselo founded Mbuso Management Solutions in January 2003 as a motivation, training and consulting company. Two decades later it is a strategic partner to government, state-owned enterprises, listed companies and communities.

Along the way we learned that motivation on its own does not change results. People need practical, accredited skills, and they need somewhere to use them. That is why we built the Itireleng Learning and Manufacturing Hub, where certified talent moves directly into our own industrial production lines.

Our head office is in Pretoria, Gauteng. Our operations are anchored in Matlosana, Dr Kenneth Kaunda District, North West. We are a Premium Client of the Small Enterprise Development Agency, and the Industrial Development Corporation supports the equipping of the factories we run.

Our model

Holistic development, from personal mastery to enterprise

Our development model starts with the person. Personal mastery is the foundation, technical skills make people employable, and business skills grow leaders and entrepreneurs. Spiritual enrichment anchors it all.

In strategy work we balance the technical side of planning with the human side of change, so people are not left behind as organisations evolve. Then we stay to help clients implement.

Founder and CEO

Daddy Khuselo

Business strategist, published author, organisational development practitioner, training expert and industrialist.

DK began as an electrical engineer and has spent his career connecting human capital development with sustainable economic projects, so that growth is inclusive and people are not left behind. He has advised the Nelson Mandela Foundation, Anglo American, Gold Fields, the National Youth Development Agency, the Gauteng Gambling Board and SALGA.

  • National Diploma in Electrical Engineering, Tshwane University of Technology
  • Programme in Business Administration, University of Johannesburg
  • Advanced Programme in Organisational Development, UNISA
  • Mastering Strategy Programme, Gordon Institute of Business Science
  • Author of The Rivonia Junction (2017), a guide to turning a passion into a thriving career and business
  • Former ministerial appointee to the council of Johannesburg Central College (2014 to 2019)
